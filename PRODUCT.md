# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leads and potential consulting clients evaluating David Villegas Sandoval. They need to understand his contribution, inspect real work and find a contact channel in a short visit.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal portfolio for a Senior Software Engineer. Make experience assessable through selected work, concrete decisions and visible product evidence.

David is open to both senior employment and consulting. Confirmed contact: david@dvillegas.cl. Company experience is described through responsibilities; the user does not retain employer artifacts or independently verifiable impact metrics.

## Brand Personality

Precise, personal, experienced. Preserve the approved personal dossier direction in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-04-portfolio-atlas-design.md`.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS marketing, anonymous technology badges, invented success metrics, simulated product screenshots, oversized repetitive sections and vague consulting copy.

## Design Principles

- Present David first, then show work before explaining process.
- Present each product's full scope using the approved descriptions; never reduce it to one illustrative feature. Keep two featured products and disclose the other products' detail on demand.
- Distinguish functional evidence from commercial or clinical outcomes.
- Project images and visit links lead to each product's homepage, where the visitor can explore capabilities and access the application.
- Keep both languages and existing section anchors usable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable at 320px, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic disclosures, sufficient contrast and meaningful captions. Product screenshots contain public or explicitly fictitious demonstration data.
